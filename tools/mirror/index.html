The launcher fills a ring from LED 1 clockwise, like a knob's travel; OXI hasn't confirmed the physical order yet, so if the rings here run the other way round than on the E16, flip it here (kept in this browser).

Mouse: scroll or drag up/down on a knob to turn it, click to push, hold to keep it pushed; hold the “O” at the top left for Shift. Keyboard: Tab to a knob, arrows to turn, Space to push.

Anything that can send MIDI to the E16 can watch and drive it while the mirror is built in. · Demo
